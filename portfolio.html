About

I'm an ambitious front-end developer with experience in data analysis and QA testing. I have built projects in React, leveraging TypeScript for type-safety, and using state management tools such as Zustand, Redux, and RTK. I am proficient in styling with CSS, SCSS, and Tailwind CSS.

With a Bachelor's degree in Computer Information Technology from BYU-I, I am currently a Software Engineer at In Time Tec, and have been with them for over 3.5 years.

Hours

While working full time, I still find the time to code.

I'm a firm believer in Malcom Gladwell's 10,000 hours ideology and aspire to hit that benchmark. In fact I log my hours whenever I code so that I can make that dream a reality.

I spend my coding time working on personal projects that solve real problems I encounter or creating tools tied to my hobbies. I also enjoy learning new technologies and strengthening my skills as I explore them. Lastly, I love taking on match-me challenges—where you're given a UI/UX mockup and tasked with recreating it perfectly while keeping it fully responsive.
